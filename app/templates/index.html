Greece Visa 10-Print Biometric Protocol

Schengen Visa Information System (VIS) Protocol

1. Passport Intake: First capture and lock applicant's passport demographics.
2. Right Four Slap: Capture Right Index, Middle, Ring, Little (NFIQ 1–2 Pass, 3 Marginal, 4–5 Recapture).
3. Left Four Slap: Capture Left Little, Ring, Middle, Index with identical quality threshold.
4. Dual Thumbs: Capture Left Thumb & Right Thumb simultaneously.
5. Central Database Storage: Atomic enrollment into database collections.
6. AFIS Matcher: 1:1 Verification against passport record or 1:N Border Control scan.
